## Product and visual contract

IdeaMotion turns educational ideas into 60–90 second motion-graphics explainers with AI narration and synchronized captions. Four creator stages only: Idea → Plan → Video → Publish. Vertical 9:16 and horizontal 16:9, 1080p MP4. Portfolio project, $0; public prepared samples, owner-approved live generation, clearly simulated publishing when live integration is unavailable.

Premium bright, spacious creative studio. Violet #7254D8, peach #FFC7AC, lavender #F0EAFB, plum text #302447, mauve border #DCCFEF. Manrope headings and Inter body with reliable fallback fonts. Use clear hierarchy, restrained gradients, readable diagrams, rounded cards, responsive stacking and accessible labels/focus/error states. No black/graphite body backgrounds. No reliance on animation or external images for initial page visibility.
